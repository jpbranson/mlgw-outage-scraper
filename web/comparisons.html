Matched reference period

Each side contributes one actual snapshot nearest the half hour, within 15 minutes. Paired capture times differ by at most 10 minutes. The medians below use the same hours on both sides; they differ from the hourly peaks above.

Reference weather must be below both thresholds: estimated regional rain <0.1 mm and airport sustained wind <10 m/s. Each exposure ends before its outcome hour; the selected additional lag applies to both periods. Low estimates do not prove dry weather everywhere. Six pairs are required for a median.

Hours within a storm are dependent. These descriptive contrasts do not control prior storms, maintenance, heat, demand or other differences, and do not estimate outage causes.
